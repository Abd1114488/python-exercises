       HOW TO PLAY
- Type a command from the menu and press Enter.
- To travel: type move, press Enter, then type the room name for example shop and press Enter.
- Pick up things with collect. Buy things in the shop with buy.
- Use save to save your progress. You can continue next time.
- Goal: bring clean water back to the village. Collect the right items,
  go to the Village and type fix. There are 3 different ways to win.
